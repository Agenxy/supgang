# Supgang paired mark: concept v1

Two equal curved counterparts suggest paired computers staying in touch. Navy and
vermilion on warm ivory connect the proposal to Agenxy's visual palette.

## Status

Design proposal, generated with the built-in image-generation tool and refined
once. The PNG is concept artwork, not a vector master or installed system icon.
The generated image contains subtle tonal variation; exact flat brand colors,
optical corrections at small sizes, monochrome treatment, and native macOS/Linux
icon packaging remain production work. No runtime or fleet settings changed.

Intended palette: ivory `#f9f2e6`, navy `#21293a`, vermilion `#a82b1f`.
The image generator is a design-time tool, not a Supgang runtime dependency.

## Initial prompt

Create one exceptional, distinctive flat vector-style brand mark for Supgang, an independent open-source utility that lets paired computers find each other when their network addresses change. This is a finished icon design, not a sheet of options, mockup or presentation.

Concept: "still connected." An abstract lowercase s made from two substantial interlocking open links, or two closely paired rounded geometric forms with a clever continuous S-shaped negative space between them. The two forms represent equal peers, neither a central server nor a hub. The result should feel like a compact confident little signal or friendly greeting, technically precise and quietly memorable. A strong unique silhouette, balanced optical weight, slightly asymmetric rhythm. Think identity work for an excellent independent engineering studio, not generic networking clip art. Favor broad shapes over thin lines, and beautiful intentional negative space. Design readable at 16px and 32px.

Composition: square 1024x1024 canvas, a single centered symbol occupying approximately 65% of the canvas width with generous clear margins. Flat warm ivory background #f9f2e6, main shape deep ink navy #21293a, the smaller complementary peer element muted vermilion #a82b1f. Only these three solid colors. Crisp smooth contours, disciplined consistent curvature. No typography, no wordmark, no letters written separately, no captions, no border, no baked-in rounded square mask, no shadow, no gradient, no 3D, no texture, no glass effects. No stock share icon, generic three-dot network, wifi arcs, globe, cloud, shield, padlock, lighthouse or infinity symbol. It should work as a standalone symbol in a system icon and in one color. Deliver one clean polished master image.

## Refinement prompt

Refine this Supgang logo concept into a clean flat geometric logo. Preserve its navy and vermilion paired curved forms and centered composition, but correct the geometry. Exactly TWO congruent rounded hook shapes related by a 180-degree rotation: navy upper-right hook and vermilion lower-left hook. Each shape is a single smooth thick bend with two rounded ends, like a softly squared capital C turned sideways. Remove the extra middle prong/finger from the red shape entirely. There must be no hand-like shapes or branching. Equal weight and equal size, two equal peers. Together their carefully separated contours create a broad flowing S-shaped negative space. Simple memorable silhouette at tiny icon sizes, roughly square overall footprint. The entire background must be an opaque uniform warm ivory #f9f2e6, NOT black or transparent. Navy solid #21293a and vermilion solid #a82b1f. Absolutely uniform flat fills without any shading, gradients, noisy edges, shadows or highlights. Hard clean vector-style antialiasing. Center symbol with 20% canvas margin on all four sides. No text, wordmark, border or rounded square app mask. Single finished symbol on square ivory canvas.
