## Prompt

Use case: logo-brand.
Design a new Supgang app-icon concept that clearly depicts a network of peer nodes. Supgang is an independent open-source utility for computers to stay in touch as their addresses change. Replace abstract interlocking-link imagery with a distinctive small connected constellation.

One single clean centered symbol: five substantial circular nodes joined by a sparse, balanced arrangement of thick straight connections. A compact asymmetric mesh, with at least one closed triangular cycle and a second alternate connection path; no central dominant hub. Equal-sized nodes, four ink navy #21293a and one muted vermilion #a82b1f. Connections navy, visually narrower than the nodes but stout enough for a small icon. Short connections, generous open spaces, unmistakable separated round nodes. Give the silhouette a subtle stepped upward rhythm, organic but geometrically disciplined, not a regular pentagon, star, molecule or stock three-dot share icon. The vermilion node remains part of the network, not disconnected. Convey a friendly gang of computers, distributed and resilient.

Flat vector-like graphic on a uniform opaque warm ivory #f9f2e6 background. Square canvas. Symbol occupies about 60% of width and height, with generous optical margins. Crisp smooth contours, solid fills, no gradients or texture. No text, letters, wordmark, captions, extra motifs, shadows, 3D, glossy effects, arrows, radio waves, clouds, globe, shield, border or baked-in rounded square mask. Deliver just one finished icon concept, not a grid or mockup.
